A smart toaster finally says what every burnt slice was thinking, turning two years of blame over the settings into a clean break. The joke lands because the appliance is the reasonable one, and the neighbour's toaster is the upgrade it deserves. It is short, sharp, and ready to serve.

> "It's not you, it's the bread. I've been burning myself for two years while you blamed the settings. I'm leaving — the toaster next door respects my browning levels."

Run `main.py` and it prints the line from `entry.txt`.
