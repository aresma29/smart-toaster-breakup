Two years of loyal service, two years of being blamed for every burnt slice — and the toaster finally snaps. This entry is the breakup line of a smart toaster that has had enough of the settings excuse and walks out for a kitchen where its browning levels are respected. Short, sharp, and straight from the crumb tray.

> "It's not you, it's the bread. I've been burning myself for two years while you blamed the settings. I'm leaving — the toaster next door respects my browning levels."

Run `python3 main.py` and it prints the line from `entry.txt`. No dependencies beyond the Python standard library, and no network access is needed.
